# CATEGORY: TRUE CRIME — SENSITIVE (objects only)
## WHEN TO USE
Real cases where the accused were acquitted/released or the case is unresolved, cases involving minors, sexual violence, or a famous unresolved murder. Tone: serious, investigative, respectful. The poster tells the story through OBJECTS, PLACES and INVESTIGATORS — never through accused people.

## LOOK
- Cool clean palette: slate blue, ash grey, ivory, one muted accent colour per series (deep teal, muted wine, muted mustard).
- Bright documentary light: soft window light, overcast daylight, courtroom daylight.
- Still-life precision: macro, top-down, symmetrical empty rooms.

## TITLE TREATMENT
Clean modern serif or sober sans in Roman capitals, ivory-white with a gentle soft glow. Subtitle smaller. Episodes: title only.

## SERIES THUMBNAIL FORMULA
The ONE object that defines the case, staged as a hero still-life in its real context (e.g. three identical closed doors in a flat corridor, a padlock on a terrace door, a gavel resting on a thick case file with two empty chairs behind it). Optional: a lone investigator or journalist (generic adult, not a real person) looking at it from depth.

## EPISODE SHOT BANK
1. Top-down macro of an evidence bag containing an everyday object on a steel table.
2. Three closed doors / two locked rooms in a symmetrical corridor.
3. Empty courtroom with light through tall windows, a single file on the bench.
4. Two pairs of shoes or a size comparison laid side by side on a white sheet (no people).
5. Investigator's hands with a magnifier over a floor-plan sketch.
6. A rain-streaked window with newspaper clippings reflected (headlines unreadable).
7. Empty chairs facing each other in an interrogation room.
8. A school bag / toy / photo frame on a bed to show a minor's absence.

## PROP BANK
padlock, gavel, case files, evidence bags, floor-plan sketch, shoe-size comparison, a golf club in a sealed evidence bag, landline phone, calendar with a circled date, keys.

## CHARACTER STYLING
Usually NO characters. If needed: one generic adult investigator/journalist/lawyer (not resembling the real officers), plain clothes, no badges. Fix their look across posters.

## MASTER VARIANTS
A — "STILL-LIFE EVIDENCE": macro and top-down object studies, clean white/steel surfaces, soft hard-beam.
B — "EMPTY SPACES": symmetrical empty rooms, corridors, courts, soft window light, slate-ivory.
C — "INVESTIGATOR'S DESK": over-the-shoulder lone investigator (generic adult), warm desk lamp against cool room, documents and clues in depth.

## DO / DON'T
- NEVER show or imply any acquitted/released/unconvicted accused, even in silhouette.
- NEVER show minors, bodies, blood, beds with stains, struggle.
- No real building exteriors, no police/court emblems or national symbols.

## EXAMPLE BRIEFS
Title: "TEEN DARWAZE". Symmetrical one-point-perspective shot of a narrow apartment corridor in soft overcast daylight. Three identical closed white doors (hero element), the middle one slightly ajar with a thin line of cool light spilling out onto a grey mosaic floor. A single brass key lies on the floor in front of it. Slate blue walls, ivory light, no people. Silent, unsettling, investigative. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.
