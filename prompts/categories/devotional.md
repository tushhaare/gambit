# CATEGORY: DEVOTIONAL
## WHEN TO USE
Festivals, vrat kathas, folk faith stories, a goddess or saint's blessing, miracles of devotion (e.g. Chhath, Teej, Karva Chauth, a village deity's katha). Tone: serene, hopeful, sacred, warm-hearted. Never fearful.

## LOOK
- Palette: rose-pink, lilac, pearl white, soft turquoise water, a touch of pale gold. NO orange/saffron wash (orange only as tiny diya-flame accents).
- Sun = a gentle white-gold disc with a soft halo, never a harsh orange ball.
- Light: dawn or golden-blue twilight, glowing mist over water, soft bloom, light rays, floating petals or fine sparkles.
- Texture: silky, luminous, painterly-real (hyper-real people, slightly dreamy air). Bright overall.

## TITLE TREATMENT
Elegant smooth serif or soft calligraphic Roman capitals in pearl-white with a pale-gold inner glow and a faint rose halo. Series thumbnail: big main title + smaller subtitle between two thin ornamental gold lines. Episodes: only the episode title, same font.

## SERIES THUMBNAIL FORMULA
Pick the story's MOST sacred moment and stage it as one luminous scene: the main devotee (named character from the character sheet) in that exact moment (e.g. standing waist-deep in the river at dawn offering arghya with a bamboo soop of fruits), with the divine presence suggested as a soft feminine figure of light / a light-burst in the sky (graceful, living, not an idol). Optional collage version: central devotee + 2–3 small soft-edged vignettes of key story beats + a water/landscape strip at the bottom. Main title + subtitle.

## EPISODE SHOT BANK (choose a different one per episode)
1. Devotee waist-deep in the river at sunrise, hands raised with an offering (hero prop: bamboo soop / brass lota).
2. Close-up of hands lighting a single diya on a riverbank step, reflection in turquoise water.
3. An empty wooden cradle under a mango tree with a soft beam of light on it (longing for a child — never show the baby).
4. A worried woman at a village courtyard doorway at twilight, a lone diya in her palm.
5. Top-down shot of a woven basket of thekua, sugarcane, coconut and fruits on a pearl cloth.
6. A goddess-like figure of soft light rising over mist behind a kneeling devotee (wide, low angle).
7. A long line of glowing floating diyas on the river at blue hour, one woman in the foreground.
8. Old grandmother telling the katha, warm face, folded hands, lilac dusk behind.
9. Return-of-blessing scene: a smiling couple at the ghat, sky bursting with soft white-gold light.

## PROP BANK
bamboo soop, brass lota, single diya, sugarcane stalks, thekua basket, coconut, marigold garland (tiny accent only), red-bordered cotton saree, glass bangles, empty cradle, folded letter, wet footprints on steps.

## CHARACTER STYLING
Village/small-town North Indian look. Women in cotton sarees (yellow-pink, red-bordered, pastel) with the pallu over the head; men in kurta-dhoti or kurta-pyjama with a gamchha. Keep each named character's face, age, hair and saree/kurta colour fixed across all posters.

## MASTER VARIANTS
A — "DAWN GHAT GLOW": sunrise over river, turquoise water, pearl mist, white-gold sun disc, wide-to-medium shots, rose-pink sky.
B — "TWILIGHT DIYA LILAC": blue-lilac dusk, diya flames as tiny warm accents, tighter emotional close-ups, soft bokeh of lamps.
C — "CELESTIAL LIGHT-BURST": divine feel — soft light rays from above, floating petals and sparkles, lower hero angles, more pearl-gold haze, painterly-epic air.

## DO / DON'T
- DO show faith through people, water, light, offerings.
- DON'T show idols, statues, temple shikhar close-ups, religious symbols (om, swastik, trishul), calendar-art gods, or any community markers.
- DON'T show children; show their presence through an empty cradle, small anklets, a toy.
- DON'T make it dark or scary; even sorrow scenes stay bright and hopeful.

## EXAMPLE BRIEFS
Title: "CHHATHI MAIYA KI KRIPA". Wide low-angle shot at dawn. RANI (34, warm wheatish face, large kind eyes, centre-parted black hair in a low bun, yellow-pink cotton saree with a red border, pallu over her head) stands waist-deep in calm turquoise river water, both arms raised holding a bamboo soop (hero prop) piled with sugarcane, coconut and thekua, offering it to the rising sun. The sun is a soft white-gold disc with a rose-pink halo; pearl mist drifts on the water; behind her in the sky a graceful feminine figure made only of soft light blesses her. Serene, sacred, hopeful. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.

Title: "SOONA PALNA". Medium shot inside a mud-plastered village courtyard at lilac twilight. An empty carved wooden cradle (hero prop) hangs from a neem branch, gently swaying, with a soft beam of pale-gold light falling only on it. RANI (34, same face, yellow-pink saree) sits on the threshold behind it, chin on her knees, eyes wet but hopeful, one diya glowing beside her foot. Pastel lilac sky, soft bokeh of distant lamps. Quiet longing, bright and gentle. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.
