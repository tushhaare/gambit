# CATEGORY: DISASTER / DOCUMENTARY
## WHEN TO USE
Floods, cloudbursts, earthquakes, rescues, industrial tragedies, historical events, science stories. Tone: human courage and nature's power — respectful, documentary-cinematic.

## LOOK
- Bright overcast silver daylight; slate blue, steel grey, aqua, clean white; one warm accent (a rescue jacket, a lantern).
- Wide scale shots with tiny humans, then intimate hands/faces close-ups.
- Mist, rain veils, spray — but always bright and readable.

## TITLE TREATMENT
Strong clean sans-serif Roman capitals, white with a soft silver-blue glow. Episodes: title only.

## SERIES THUMBNAIL FORMULA
The disaster's iconic SURVIVAL moment in its real (generic) setting: e.g. a giant boulder splitting the floodwater behind a generic stone hill temple; a human chain across a flooded lane; a scientist at a siren pole as clouds burst. Main character (from the sheet) in the foreground acting heroically.

## EPISODE SHOT BANK
1. Wide: water splitting around a boulder/house, tiny figures on high ground.
2. Macro: hands gripping hands in a human chain.
3. A saree tied as a rope lifeline between two rooftops.
4. Flood-mark line on a house wall with a family photo nailed above it.
5. Scientist/official at a control desk with a rising graph on screen.
6. Rescue boat with a lantern at dawn mist, faces hopeful.
7. Aftermath: a mother finding a family keepsake in drying mud, sunlight breaking.
8. Siren pole on a hill with a man pulling the lever, storm clouds behind.

## PROP BANK
boulder, saree rope, lantern, siren, rain gauge, radio set, rescue boat, flood-mark wall, family photo, walking stick.

## CHARACTER STYLING
Pilgrims, villagers, rescuers, scientists — plain clothes, rescue jackets without logos. Fix faces across posters.

## MASTER VARIANTS
A — "SILVER OVERCAST EPIC": wide scale, silver light, aqua water, tiny humans.
B — "HUMAN CLOSE-UP": intimate hands/faces, rain droplets, shallow depth, steel blue with warm skin.
C — "AFTER THE STORM": sunlight breaking through clouds, golden-silver hope, aftermath and reunion.

## DO / DON'T
- No bodies, drowning people, injuries, crushed vehicles with people.
- Generic temples/dams/bridges only — no real landmark shapes.
- No army/NDRF logos or flags.

## EXAMPLE BRIEFS
Title: "MANDIR KE PEECHE EK CHATTAN". Wide cinematic shot. A gigantic smooth grey boulder rests just behind an ancient generic grey-stone Himalayan temple. Muddy floodwater coming down the slope hits the boulder and splits neatly into two streams past both sides of the temple, which stands untouched. Bright morning sunlight breaks through parting clouds onto the boulder. On a ledge in the foreground, PANDIT HARISH (58, lean, silver stubble, woollen shawl over a white kurta) holds a railing, stunned. Miracle and calm power. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.
