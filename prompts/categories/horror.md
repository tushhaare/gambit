# CATEGORY: HORROR / SUPERNATURAL
## WHEN TO USE
Hauntings, possession, curses, ghost stories, unexplained events, "khauf" stories. Tone: elegant quiet dread — suspense, not gore.

## LOOK
- Pale mint, ivory, misty teal, grey-blue; small maroon or old-gold accents.
- BRIGHT soft light: overcast, foggy daylight, moonlit-but-bright interiors, glowing windows.
- Dread comes from composition: emptiness, reflections, a doorway, a shape in mist, something slightly wrong.

## TITLE TREATMENT
Thin elegant serif Roman capitals in ivory with a cold mint glow, very slight soft blur halo. Smooth — never dripping or scratched. Episodes: title only.

## SERIES THUMBNAIL FORMULA
The haunting's signature image in its real place: e.g. an empty wheelchair facing a glowing window in a long hospital corridor, a reflection in glass that does not match, a white veiled figure standing protectively behind the family at the house gate. Main adult character from the character sheet shown reacting.

## EPISODE SHOT BANK
1. Reflection in a mirror/glass door showing a faint pale shape (no face).
2. An empty wheelchair / swing / rocking chair moving slightly (child presence without a child).
3. A woman holding a single diya in a long dim corridor, light on her face.
4. A protective white-saree figure in mist at a doorway (calm, not menacing).
5. Top-down of a bed with an indentation and scattered marigold petals.
6. Fogged window with handprints from outside.
7. A calm elder with a brass lamp and calm face (no ritual symbols).
8. Family huddled together on a staircase looking up at a light in the attic.

## PROP BANK
single diya, mirror, empty wheelchair, swing, old trunk, photograph frame, wind chime, white dupatta in the wind, antique clock, lantern.

## CHARACTER STYLING
Ordinary middle-class Indian family look. Haunting child characters are NEVER shown. Ghostly figures are faceless or veiled, pale, calm. Fix living characters' faces across posters.

## MASTER VARIANTS
A — "PALE MINT STILLNESS": foggy bright daylight, mint/ivory, empty spaces, symmetric frames.
B — "MOONLIT HOUSE": cool silver-blue moonlit interiors with one warm diya, faces clearly lit.
C — "REFLECTIONS & GLASS": glass, mirrors, water reflections; teal-grey with old-gold accents; off-centre framing.

## DO / DON'T
- No blood, possessed-face distortion, wounds, corpses, self-harm, suicide imagery.
- No children on screen; no scary faces on the ghost.
- No religious symbols or exorcism rituals; no real hospitals/haveli names.

## EXAMPLE BRIEFS
Title: "SHEESHE MEIN KOI AUR". Long hospital corridor in soft cool blue-white glow, main lights dimmed but faces bright. MEERA (40, round gentle face, worried eyes, short-sleeved pastel-green cotton saree, hair in a loose bun) pushes an empty wheelchair toward a large glass door. In the glass reflection (hero element) the wheelchair seems to carry a faint pale indistinct shape, no face visible. Meera freezes, staring at it. Mint-teal palette, eerie and clean. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.
