# CATEGORY: HEIST / ROBBERY
## WHEN TO USE
Bank or train robberies, cons, smuggling, big-money capers. Tone: slick, clever, suspenseful — like a premium heist film.

## LOOK
- Luminous blue-hour palette: electric blue, cyan, steel, with hot accents (cutter sparks, a sodium lamp, a red signal).
- Precise geometric framing, top-down and from-below angles, reflections, light streaks.
- Bright and glossy; night is luminous, never murky.

## TITLE TREATMENT
Sleek wide sans-serif Roman capitals, cool white with an electric-blue glow edge, slightly metallic but smooth. Episodes: title only.

## SERIES THUMBNAIL FORMULA
The heist's signature move in its exact setting: e.g. a hole cut in a train coach roof seen from inside looking up at blue sky with a gloved hand lowering in; a vault door half open with light spilling; a mastermind (only if convicted) silhouetted with a map. One hero element, clear storytelling.

## EPISODE SHOT BANK
1. Looking up through a cut hole in a metal roof at a blue-hour sky (hero: the hole).
2. Gloved hands with a cutter throwing sparks on steel, macro.
3. Satellite/GPS screen glow on a planner's face in a dark-blue room (bright face).
4. Wooden trunks of sealed currency bundles in a moving train coach (generic, unmarked).
5. Investigators at a railway yard looking at an empty coach at dawn.
6. Top-down of a hand-drawn route map with coins as markers and a torch beam.
7. Rooftop of a moving train under a moonlit blue sky (wide, generic train).
8. Interrogation/arrest aftermath: a table with seized tools in evidence bags.

## PROP BANK
gas cutter, hand-drawn route map, torch, gloves, sealed wooden trunks, currency bundles (unmarked), walkie-talkie, timetable, satellite screen.

## CHARACTER STYLING
Gang members only if convicted; otherwise use gloved hands, silhouettes from behind, or investigators. Plain uniforms without railway/police insignia. Fixed looks across posters.

## MASTER VARIANTS
A — "BLUE-HOUR PRECISION": cyan/steel blue hour, sparks as accent, geometric top-down and from-below angles.
B — "SODIUM NIGHT TRAIN": warm sodium-vapour orange-amber lamps (small accents) vs deep teal, motion streaks, cinematic rail mood.
C — "INVESTIGATION DAYLIGHT": bright cold morning light, aftermath and detective angle, steel grey with one red accent.

## DO / DON'T
- No guns aimed, no assault, no injured guards.
- No real railway/bank logos, liveries, uniforms with insignia, or real station names.
- Currency bundles are generic, no readable notes.

## EXAMPLE BRIEFS
Title: "CHHAT MEIN SURAAKH". Dramatic from-below shot inside a dim but well-lit steel railway mail coach. A neat rectangular hole (hero element) has been cut in the metal roof; through it shines a luminous blue-hour sky with a thin crescent moon. Orange cutter sparks still drift down in the air, frozen mid-fall. Sealed wooden trunks stand stacked below. A gloved hand reaches in through the hole's edge. Cyan and steel palette with hot spark accents, bright and crisp, suspenseful. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.
