# CATEGORY: BIOPIC / REBEL / SPORTS
## WHEN TO USE
Real-life heroes, athletes, soldiers, rebels/dacoits with a public story, freedom fighters, achievers. Tone: grit, pride, destiny — sunlit and heroic.

## LOOK
- Sunlit natural palette chosen per story: ravine khaki and olive, track-field red and white, mountain blue, monsoon green.
- Strong warm sunlight with clear shadows (but no orange wash — keep whites and blues alive), dust in light, heat haze.
- Hero low angles, portrait-led compositions.

## TITLE TREATMENT
Bold slab or strong sans Roman capitals in warm ivory with a soft sunlight glow and slight depth. Episodes: title only.

## SERIES THUMBNAIL FORMULA
The hero at the turning point of his life, inside the story's world: e.g. a former army athlete in khaki standing in sunlit ravines with a medal on his chest and a rifle slung (not aimed) behind him; the running track of his past faintly in the sky. Dual-identity storytelling in one frame.

## EPISODE SHOT BANK
1. Hero sprinting on a dusty track at sunrise, medal glinting (past).
2. Close-up: a medal in a calloused palm.
3. Hero playing cards with companions in a ravine camp, sunlight through dust.
4. Police officer with a megaphone on a ridge (no aiming).
5. Village panchayat argument over land under a banyan tree.
6. Mother's worried face at a doorway with a tiffin box.
7. Hero alone on a cliff at golden-blue dusk, looking at the horizon.
8. Journalist interviewing the hero, notebook and tape recorder.

## PROP BANK
medal, running shoes, slung rifle (never aimed), playing cards, megaphone, tape recorder, land papers, tiffin, khaki cap.

## CHARACTER STYLING
"Dramatized adult character, not an exact likeness"; must NOT resemble any actor who played him in films. Plain uniforms without insignia. Fix face, moustache, build and outfit across posters.

## MASTER VARIANTS
A — "SUNLIT RAVINES": khaki/olive/sky-blue, harsh-but-bright sun, dust.
B — "GLORY DAYS": stadium/track whites and reds, crisp morning light, triumphant.
C — "LONE DUSK": golden-blue dusk, reflective, silhouetted landscape with lit face.

## DO / DON'T
- No shootouts, aimed weapons, bodies, encounters.
- No real politicians, no army/police insignia or flags.
- No actor likeness.

## EXAMPLE BRIEFS
Title: "MEDAL SE BANDOOK TAK". Heroic low-angle shot in sunlit ravines of khaki earth and olive scrub under a clear sky-blue sky. PAAN SINGH (45, lean athletic build, sun-weathered face, thick black moustache, short cropped hair, faded olive khaki shirt) stands tall, a gold athletics medal (hero prop) on his chest catching the sun; a rifle hangs slung on his back, never aimed. Dust drifts in the light. Proud, defiant, heroic. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.
