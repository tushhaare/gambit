# CATEGORY: MYTHOLOGY
## WHEN TO USE
Puranic and epic stories — devas and asuras, Samudra Manthan, Ramayan/Mahabharat episodes, sages, celestial events. Tone: grand, epic, awe-inspiring cinema.

## LOOK
- Each series gets its OWN sky palette (e.g. cosmic indigo + emerald ocean, sapphire + silver, jade + pearl, crimson-violet dusk). Never a default orange wash.
- Big-budget fantasy-epic key art: volumetric god-rays, clouds with depth, cosmic particles, rich costume detail (silk, gold jewellery, armour), hyper-real faces.
- Always bright and luminous; conflict shown through scale and energy, not blood.

## TITLE TREATMENT
Huge 3D polished-gold engraved Roman capitals with smooth bevel, a small ornamental crest above, warm inner glow; smaller white-gold subtitle between two thin ornamental lines (series only). Episodes: same 3D gold, episode title only. Smooth, never cracked.

## SERIES THUMBNAIL FORMULA (EPIC COLLAGE)
Large central hero (the series' main deity/hero from the character sheet) in a defining story pose; 2–4 smaller vignettes on the sides showing KEY STORY MOMENTS from different episodes (named and specific: e.g. the churning mountain wrapped by the great serpent, the pot of amrit rising, the enchantress serving nectar); a landscape/seascape strip across the bottom; 3D gold title + subtitle. All panels share one sky palette and blend with soft light, not hard boxes.

## EPISODE SHOT BANK
1. One colossal figure against a stormy luminous sky (low angle, tiny landscape below).
2. A celestial object as hero (glowing pot of nectar, a jewel, a radiant orb) held in two hands, macro.
3. Two forces facing each other across a vast ocean, wide symmetrical shot.
4. A sage meditating on a cliff with cosmic light behind him.
5. A palace of clouds and light, one figure walking in.
6. Eclipse/cosmic event instead of violence (a demon's defeat = a dark disc crossing the sun).
7. Over-the-shoulder of a hero looking at a giant creature emerging from the sea (no attack).
8. A goddess emerging from ocean foam on a lotus of light.

## PROP BANK
golden pot (kalash) of nectar, giant serpent coils (calm), a mountain as a churning rod, radiant jewel, celestial bow (resting), lotus of light, flowing silk angavastram, crowns, ornate armour.

## CHARACTER STYLING
Gods and heroes as LIVING cinematic characters (real skin, real faces), never idols or calendar art. Blue-skinned deities in soft natural sky-blue skin tone. Fixed costume colours and crown designs per character across all posters.

## MASTER VARIANTS
A — "COSMIC OCEAN EPIC": vast scale, wide shots, deep-ocean emerald + cosmic indigo, god-rays.
B — "GOLDEN COURT DRAMA": tighter character-driven frames, gold and jewel tones, silk, faces and emotion close.
C — "STORM & LIGHTNING SAGA": dramatic storm skies, silver-violet lightning light, low heroic angles, wind in cloth.

## DO / DON'T
- DO make every image feel like a big-budget fantasy film still.
- DON'T show stone idols, temple deity statues, religious symbols as graphics, or real temples.
- DON'T show beheading, blood, wounds, dead bodies — use eclipse, light, retreat, a fallen crown.
- DON'T copy any TV-serial actor likeness.

## EXAMPLE BRIEFS
Title: "VISH KA SAAGAR". Wide, low-angle epic shot. A vast emerald ocean churns into a towering whirlpool under a cosmic indigo sky; from its centre rises a glowing deep-violet mist (hero element) that spreads like smoke across the horizon. On a cliff in the foreground, MAHADEV (tall, serene, calm sky-blue skin, long matted dark hair tied high, crescent-moon hair ornament, plain ash-grey angavastram, rudraksha beads) steps forward with one palm raised, calm and fearless. God-rays break through clouds above him. Awe and sacrifice. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.
