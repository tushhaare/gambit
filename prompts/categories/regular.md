# CATEGORY: REGULAR / DRAMA
## WHEN TO USE
Family dramas, romance, social stories, self-help, inspirational life stories, comedy-drama. Tone: emotional, relatable, warm.

## LOOK
- Soft bright natural daylight; pastel or fresh palette chosen per series (peach-mint, sky-blue & cream, sage & blush, lavender & ivory).
- Shallow depth, clean backgrounds, real Indian homes, streets, offices.
- Emotional single scene per image.

## TITLE TREATMENT
Friendly smooth rounded serif or soft sans Roman capitals, white with a gentle colour glow from the series palette. Episodes: title only.

## SERIES THUMBNAIL FORMULA
The central relationship/conflict of the story in one moment: the two main characters (from the sheet) in the story's key place with the story's hero prop between them (a letter, a rented flat key, a wedding card, a phone).

## EPISODE SHOT BANK
1. Two characters on a balcony at golden-blue evening, not looking at each other.
2. Close-up of a hand holding a letter/phone with a crucial message.
3. Kitchen confrontation, steam from a pressure cooker, soft window light.
4. Rain at a bus stop, one umbrella.
5. Office cubicle late afternoon, a resignation letter.
6. Happy reunion hug at a railway platform (generic), light flare.
7. Top-down of a dining table with one empty plate.
8. Old parents on a charpai under a neem tree, looking at a photo.

## PROP BANK
letter, phone, keys, wedding card, umbrella, suitcase, photo frame, tiffin, scooter.

## CHARACTER STYLING
Contemporary Indian middle-class wardrobe. Fix each character's face, hair, age and signature outfit colour across posters.

## MASTER VARIANTS
A — "SOFT DAYLIGHT": bright window light, pastel palette, medium shots.
B — "GOLDEN-BLUE EVENING": warm practical lights vs blue sky, romantic/melancholic.
C — "FRESH OUTDOORS": streets, rain, rooftops, airy wide frames.

## DO / DON'T
- Keep it hopeful and readable; no violence or harassment imagery.
- No brand logos on phones, cars, shops.

## EXAMPLE BRIEFS
Title: "TEEN HAZAAR KA UDHAAR". Medium shot on a small-town rooftop at golden-blue evening. VIKAS (29, slim, short wavy hair, light stubble, sky-blue checked shirt) holds out a folded envelope (hero prop) to his elder brother RAJESH (36, broad face, thin moustache, grey half-sleeve shirt), who refuses to look at it, arms crossed. Clothes lines and water tanks behind, soft sky gradient. Hurt pride, unspoken love. Square 1:1, title in the top 30%.
